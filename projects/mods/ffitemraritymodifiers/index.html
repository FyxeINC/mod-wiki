FFItemRarityModifiers

Modrinth ↗CurseForge ↗Source ↗

Overview

Adds configurable rarity rolls and small modifiers to gear.

Features

  • Items can receive positive and negative modifiers based on rarity when first obtained.
  • Trader offers show their final item before purchase.
  • Durable gear now includes bows, shields, fishing rods, shears, brushes, elytra, wolf armor, and riding sticks.
  • Datapacks define source chances, rarity weights, eligible item categories, and optional item-tag restrictions on modifiers.

FFUtilities 0.2.11 (0.2.15 on Minecraft 26.x) is included in this mod JAR; no separate library download is needed. Fabric players also need Fabric API.

How rarities work

Single-stack weapons, armor and tools can receive a rarity (Common, Uncommon, Rare or Epic) with small stat modifiers attached, good and bad alike.

  • Rarer items can roll more (and stronger) positive modifiers, and fewer negative ones. Common items can still roll a modifier or two.
  • A negative modifier and its positive counterpart (for example, attack damage up and attack damage down) never appear on the same item.
  • An item that received a rarity roll shows its rarity in the tooltip, including Common. Items that haven't been rolled show no rarity line. Modifiers are listed in blue for good and red-orange for bad; hold Shift for precise numbers.
  • Items can gain rarity from crafting, fishing, villager and wandering trader trades, mob drops and loot tables. The bundled sources give every eligible item a roll, weighted heavily toward Common (crafting: 200 Common, 50 Uncommon, 5 Rare, 1 Epic). Datapacks can change the chance and weights.
  • Crafting results roll the same way whether you click or Shift-click them.
  • Villager and wandering trader results are decided when an offer appears, so you can inspect the item before trading.
  • Bows, crossbows, fishing rods, shears, brushes, flint and steel, shields, elytra, wolf armor and the two riding sticks count too. Gear only rolls modifiers that match its category and any item-tag restriction; for example, fishing luck is for rods and mining speed is for mining tools and shears.
  • Automatic sources assign rarity when an item is first obtained and don't replace it later.

Configuration

The common config holds:

  • tooltip.basicLayout (normal tooltip) and tooltip.altLayout (Shift tooltip): HORIZONTAL puts rarity and modifiers on one line (like "Rare: Swift, Sturdy"), VERTICAL lists each modifier on its own line. By default the normal tooltip is one line and the Shift tooltip is a list.
  • enableTrading, enableMobDrops and enableLootTables turn those sources on or off. If you previously used tradingMode=DISABLED, set enableTrading to false instead.

Chances, weights and modifiers are datapack files; see Datapacks.

Operator command

Operators can assign a specific rarity to the item a player holds in their main hand. The command rolls fresh modifiers with that rarity's weights and replaces any rarity and modifiers this mod assigned before. The item must be an eligible single-stack weapon, armor piece, tool or opted-in accessory.

/ffitemraritymodifiers apply <common|uncommon|rare|epic> [player]

In game, omit player to target yourself. From the server console, include an online player name, for example ffitemraritymodifiers apply epic Alex.
