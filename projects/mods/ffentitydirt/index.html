FF Entity Dirt

Modrinth ↗Source ↗

Overview

Entities pick up dirt from the ground, get damage marks where they are hit, and wash both off in water.

Features

  • Moving on natural ground (soil, sand, gravel, clay, mud, snow, stone) leaves dirt low on the body in the color of the block underneath; floors like planks and carpet stay clean. Faster movement means more dirt that reaches higher; standing still adds none.
  • Fall damage splatters dirt from the bottom up, scaled by the damage taken.
  • Hits leave damage marks: projectiles where they land, melee hits on the side facing the attacker. Size and strength grow with damage, and each mob or tag can have its own color.
  • Water slowly washes dirt and damage marks off, only below the water line.
  • Dirt and damage marks are real FF Entity Painting paint on their own locked layers, each with its own on/off switch and mob filters, so hand-painted art is never washed away.
  • Server settings for mob and block whitelists and blacklists (IDs, tags or whole mods), dirt colors, messiness, rates, reach and wash speed; client settings for shape reporting.
  • Datapack tags ffentitydirt:dirty_surfaces and ffentitydirt:clean_surfaces (blocks) ffentitydirt:never_dirty and ffentitydirt:never_damaged (entity types).
