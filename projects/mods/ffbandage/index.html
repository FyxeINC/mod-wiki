FFBandage

Modrinth ↗CurseForge ↗Source ↗

Overview

A configurable healing bandage item.

Features

  • Heal with a bandage item.
  • Configure healing and use speed.

Using the bandage

  1. Craft a bandage: put paper, a stick, and paper in a row on a crafting table. You get one bandage. It is also in the Combat tab of the Creative inventory.
  2. Hold use with the bandage, just like eating. After 24 ticks (a little over a second) it heals you.
  3. Each bandage heals 1 to 2 health (half a heart to one heart) by default and is used up. In Creative mode it is not used up.

The mod is needed on both the server and the client.

Configuration

Settings live in config/ffbandage-common.toml:

Option Default What it does
healAmountMin 1 The least health a bandage heals. 2 health is one heart.
healAmountMax 2 The most health a bandage heals. Each use picks a random amount between the min and max.
healDuration 24 How long you hold the bandage before it heals, in ticks (20 ticks is one second).

For example, set both heal amounts to 6 for a sturdy bandage that always heals three hearts, and healDuration to 40 so it takes two seconds to apply. If healAmountMin ends up above healAmountMax, the mod simply swaps them.

You can also change these in game: use the config button in the Mods list on NeoForge 1.21.1 and newer, or Mod Menu on Fabric 1.21.1 and newer. On older versions, edit the file.

Replacing the recipe

The bandage recipe is a normal crafting recipe. Override data/ffbandage/recipe/bandage.json (data/ffbandage/recipes/bandage.json on 1.20.1 and 1.20.6) in a datapack with your own shaped or shapeless recipe. The mod has no other datapack options.
