FFFoodSpoilageAndPreservation

Modrinth ↗CurseForge ↗Source ↗

Food does not stay fresh forever. This mod tracks spoilage, preservation, and temperature so storage choices matter.

What changes in game

  • Food moves through Fresh, Good, Stale, Rotting, and Rotten stages. Later stages provide less hunger and saturation; fully spoiled food is replaced.
  • Preservation modifiers and server settings can slow spoilage or make food immune.
  • Temperature and storage conditions affect the rate shown in food tooltips. Hold Shift for additional tooltip details.

Food made through preservation crafting or smoking starts aging when it first enters the world.

Keeping food fresh

Storage matters. By default, an ender chest stops spoilage; barrels, pots, and bundles slow it compared with an ordinary chest or your inventory. You can also preserve food directly:

MethodHow to use it
CuredCraft food with bone meal.
BrinedCraft suitable food with an awkward potion.
CandiedCraft suitable food with a honey bottle.
SmokedUse a smoker with suitable food.
FrozenUse ice items or keep food cold. Frozen food must thaw before you can eat it.

Storage and moving food

When you open a container, spoilage uses that inventory's location and conditions. If the inventory has no known world location, neutral ambient conditions apply. Moving food accounts for time in its previous storage before using the new conditions.

Stacking food

By default, food with the same spoilage stage and preservation modifiers can stack even when individual pieces have different histories. The resulting stack keeps the worst spoilage progress. This works with inventory clicks, pickups, bundles, hoppers, and furnace outputs.

Server owners can set mergeSameStageFood to false to require identical freshness histories. With that setting, differently aged food may prevent a furnace output from stacking until the output is emptied.

Spoilage in detail

Food that is not immune gains a spoilage component and moves through named stages. Stages are datapack-defined (see Spoilage stages); the defaults are Fresh, Good, Stale, Rotting and Rotten at 1 / 2 / 3 / 2 / 2 minutes. Nutrition and saturation drop each stage. Eating restores the reduced amounts up to the normal hunger and saturation limits, without subtracting existing saturation. When the last stage fills, the stack is replaced (default rotten flesh).

Preventing or slowing spoilage

  • Never spoil: the server neverSpoil list accepts item IDs or #tags. Defaults include golden foods, rotten flesh, chorus fruit, honey bottle, #c:foods/honey and #c:foods/golden.
  • Rate 0: a rateOverrides entry id_or_tag=0 also stops spoilage.
  • Containers: the ender chest rate is 0 by default. Barrels, pots and bundles are slower than a chest or your inventory.
  • Modifiers: one per exclusive category. A new modifier replaces one from the same category only if it slows spoilage more, so Smoked beats Candied, Candied beats Brined, and Brined beats Cured. Cured: craft with bone meal. Brined: craft with an awkward potion (not sweets). Candied: craft with a honey bottle (not soups). Smoked: smoker on cooked meat and fish. Frozen: ice items, or stay cold long enough.
  • Temperature: cold biomes and body temperature slow spoilage; heat speeds it up. Frozen food can't be eaten until it thaws (hot items, a fueled Cold Sweat Boiler, or warmth).

Open-container rates follow the actual inventory, including remotely opened inventories that expose their backing container. Looking away doesn't change tooltip rates. Food keeps the rate from its last storage check for elapsed time; moving it between an ender chest and your inventory settles the old rate at the moment you move it.

Stacking rules

The server setting mergeSameStageFood defaults to true. Food with the same spoilage stage and current modifiers can combine through clicks, shift-clicks, pickups, dropped items, bundles, hoppers, and furnace or smoker outputs. All other item properties must still match.

The combined stack keeps the highest spoilage progress and oldest timestamp, so mixing in fresh food can't refresh older food. Freeze and thaw progress is averaged by item count: combining 3 items at 100 temperature-progress ticks with 1 at 300 gives 150 ticks. Only the items actually transferred count toward the average. Untracked food counts as Fresh with no modifiers and zero progress.

On NeoForge 1.21.1 this also covers other mods' inventories that use NeoForge item handlers. On Fabric, and on NeoForge 1.21.11 and 26.x (which replaced item handlers with a new transfer system), it covers everything vanilla, but other mods' storage that moves items through the loader's own transfer system stays strict: only food with identical freshness stacks there.

Set mergeSameStageFood = false in the world's serverconfig/fffoodspoilageandpreservation-server.toml to require identical freshness and freeze/thaw histories. You may then need to empty furnace or smoker outputs before cooking continues. The master spoilage toggle also disables relaxed merging.

Tooltips and visuals

Tooltips (hold Shift for more lines) show stage, modifiers, container rate, freeze/thaw progress and remaining time. Modifiers also mark food with their color: a tint (or, since 0.12.0, a gradient, radial, noise or desaturate layer from the modifier's item_visuals) covers the item with a see-through wash in inventories and in your hand, and an outline draws a 1-pixel border in inventories. Frozen uses both (icy blue); the rest tint: Brined light yellow green, Candied gold orange, Cured brown, Smoked dark grey. Several tints layer with the higher-ranked modifier in front.

Spoiled food also gets a procedural noise overlay between the item icon and any modifier tint. By default the first two stages have none; later stages are stronger, scaling evenly between a start and an end intensity, and the color blends from dark green (first noisy stage) to dark brown (last stage). It shows in inventories and in your hand.

Client settings: in the [visuals.modifiers] section, modifierVisuals (default on) turns modifier visuals on or off, modifierVisualOpacity (0 to 100, default 100) scales every layer to that percent of the opacity its datapack gives it, and modifierTintInventory / modifierTintHeld (default on) switch all non-outline layers (tints, gradients, noise and so on) off in inventories or in hands separately. The old tintOpacity option was removed in 0.12.0. The noise settings sit in the [visuals.spoilage] section: spoilageNoiseInventory and spoilageNoiseHeld (default on), spoilageNoiseStartStage (first stage with noise, one-based, default 3), spoilageNoiseStartIntensity and spoilageNoiseEndIntensity (percent strength at that stage and at the last stage, scaled evenly between; defaults 20 and 100), spoilageNoiseOpacity (0 to 100, default 80), spoilageNoiseBlend (NORMAL, MULTIPLY, SCREEN or OVERLAY; default MULTIPLY, which darkens the icon), and spoilageNoiseColorStart / spoilageNoiseColorEnd (hex RRGGBB, defaults #1F4A1A and #4A2F17).

Compatibility details

  • AppleSkin (NeoForge and Fabric): hunger and saturation overlays use spoilage-scaled values. The mod's own effective-food tooltip line is hidden so the two don't stack. Frozen food hides the AppleSkin overlay because it can't be eaten.
  • Tough As Nails (both loaders, every supported Minecraft version): carried food uses your current body temperature level, and containers use the local temperature level. Cold Sweat takes priority per read when both mods are installed. If TAN temperature is disabled or its API is unavailable, the normal biome fallback applies. ICY/COLD/NEUTRAL/WARM/HOT map to ambient proxies -0.5/0/0.3/1/2, rather than degrees. They use the existing ambient thresholds and durations, including tooltip progress: by default ICY and COLD slow spoilage and eventually freeze food, NEUTRAL leaves it alone, and WARM and HOT speed spoilage and thaw frozen food. The Cold Sweat body thresholds do not apply to TAN. TAN is optional and must be installed separately; thirst and drinks are unchanged.
  • Cold Sweat (NeoForge only; the Cold Sweat id fix applies on NeoForge generally): player body temperature can drive inventory food instead of biome air temperature. On Minecraft 1.21.1, the Icebox and Boiler take food in their waterskin slots (by hand or hopper). A fueled Icebox freezes that food and a fueled Boiler thaws it, even when nobody has them open. Without fuel they act like any other container. Server settings under temperature then coldsweat set the speeds: iceboxFreezeDurationTicks (default 1200 ticks, about a minute) and boilerThawDurationTicks (default 400 ticks, about 20 seconds). Lower is faster.
  • EMI (Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge and Fabric; EMI has no 1.21.11 or 26.x release): recipe pages for every datapack apply and remove craft, plus smoker (Smoked). Dynamic dummy recipes are hidden.

Eat effects: freshBuffs.enabled and nearSpoiledDebuffs.enabled switch on the random buffs and debuffs of every stage whose buff_chance or debuff_chance is above 0 (by default Fresh buffs and Rotten debuffs). The chances, and optionally the effect lists, are set per stage in the stage datapack; a stage without its own lists uses the freshBuffs.buffs / nearSpoiledDebuffs.debuffs config tables. The stages server group now holds only rottenReplacement and rottenOverrides; stage names, durations and base chances were removed from the config.

The in-game config screen is in the Mods list on NeoForge and, on Fabric, through Mod Menu.
