FF Crop Evaporation

Modrinth ↗Source ↗

Overview

Crop growth can consume nearby water and require water access.

Features

  • Configure evaporation chance, search radius, and weighted replacement blocks.
  • Optionally prevent growth when suitable water is absent.

FFUtilities is included in this mod JAR; no separate library download is needed.
