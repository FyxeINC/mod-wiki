Modrinth ↗CurseForge ↗Source ↗

Overview

Adds periodic personal and shared quests, rewards, and a Quest Board.

Features

  • Daily, weekly, and monthly quests track play activity.
  • A FFRandomQuests screen (opened next to the inventory recipe book) lets you pick which quests track on your HUD.
  • A HUD tracker, board, and public registries support integration.

Quest Board

Craftable block: 8 oak planks + 1 book.

Interaction Effect
Right-click (empty hand) Show active quests and claim completed rewards
Right-click (holding an item) Deliver that item toward matching Collect quests
Sneak + right-click Hand in all matching items from your inventory

Completed quests can always be claimed at the board, even if global auto-claim is off.

Main goals

Long-term objectives with no time limit, separate from daily, weekly and monthly quests.

Scope Description
Personal One per player, for example complete 15 daily quests, defeat 250 mobs, or reach adaptive rating +1.5
Server One per world: all players must finish their personal main goal (the target scales with player count)

Main goals never expire, show on the HUD (pink and red) and at the Quest Board, and give exclusive rewards (diamonds, netherite scrap, enchanted golden apples, the server legendary pool). Claim with /quests goal claim, /quests goal claimserver, or at the Quest Board. Personal goal types (configurable): complete any, daily, weekly or monthly quests; complete N of a quest type; reach an adaptive rating; claim N rewards; total kills; travel distance; survive days.

FFRandomQuests screen

Click the square Q button next to the recipe book in your inventory. It has three tabs:

Tab Shows
My Quests Your personal daily, weekly and monthly quests and your main goal
Shared Quests The server-wide shared quests and the server goal
Info Read-only details about your game state, starting with your difficulty breakdown

Each quest tab lists every category as a header card (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Main Goal or Server Goal) in one scrollable list. A header shows even when the category currently has no quests, as long as it's enabled, and counts down to the next reset. Every quest gets a card with its description, progress and a TrackOnHUD checkbox. Each header's checkbox sets the default for new quests in that category. Completed quests stay listed here even after they leave the HUD.

HUD tracker

The top-left tracker lists every unfinished quest whose TrackOnHUD box is checked, shared quests first. Completed quests drop off the list. Progress and countdown timers (showTimer) show automatically. Position, scale, colors and background are in the config. You also get a private chat message when you complete a quest, even for shared ones.

Rewards

Type Description
ITEM Any item stack
EXPERIENCE XP points
EFFECT A potion effect from a configurable list (defaults: speed, strength, regeneration, haste, resistance, fire resistance, night vision, jump boost, absorption, luck). Modded effects can be added in the config
HEAL Restore hearts
EMERALDS Emeralds as simple currency
COMMAND Run a server command (%player%, %uuid% placeholders)
LOOT_TABLE Roll a vanilla or modded loot table
RANDOM_POOL Pick one of several sub-rewards at grant time

Harder and monthly quests get bonus surprise pools and dungeon loot rolls.

Dynamic difficulty

Difficulty is computed per player when quests generate:

Factor Source
Base Config baseDifficulty
Player level XP level times a factor
World age In-game days
Play time Vanilla play-time stats (hours)
Distance Distance from world spawn
Equipment Armor and held tool tier (leather to netherite)
Dimension Bonus in the Nether and End; late-game gear in the Overworld
Deaths Soft ease after many deaths
Adaptive rating Learns from completions and failures
Period multiplier Daily x1, weekly x1.8, monthly x3 (configurable)

Completing quests (especially hard ones, quickly) raises your adaptive rating; letting quests expire lowers it. It is clamped by adaptiveMin / adaptiveMax, and final difficulty by difficultyMin / difficultyMax. The HUD title shows the rating when it isn't zero (for example Quests +0.3).
