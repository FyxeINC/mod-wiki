FFNutrition

Modrinth ↗Source ↗

Overview

Rewards diet variety with nutrition categories that affect maximum health.

Features

  • Foods add to datapack defined categories that decay over time.
  • The server computes a nutrition score and applies a max health modifier.

How nutrition works

Every player has a value from 0 to 100 for each nutrition category. Out of the box the categories are meat, fruit, veg, carbs and sweets, and everyone starts at 50 in each.

  • Eating a food raises the categories it is made of. The gain is the food's makeup multiplied by how filling it is, so bigger meals give more.
  • Every category slowly drains over time.
  • Your nutrition score is the average of all categories. Maximum health follows it: a well-rounded eater climbs toward full health, and a lopsided one settles toward the minimum.
  • A category caps at 100, so stuffing yourself with one kind of food only gets you so far.
  • Hold Shift over a food to see roughly what it is made of ("Mostly meat", "A pinch of sweets").
  • Foods can be eaten with a full hunger bar (can be turned off). Cake and other food blocks count too, using what they actually restore.
  • Experience slows the drain. Hunger and saturation can also tick down slowly on their own (configurable).

The Metabolic Gauge

Craft it from four iron ingots around any food (it's also in the Tools & Utilities creative tab). Hold it in either hand and your category percentages appear above the hotbar, color-coded from white through gray and orange to red as they run low.

Commands

Command Who What it does
/ffnutrition getNutritionDataVague <player> everyone A friendly hint about how well fed a player is and what they lack
/ffnutrition getNutritionData <player> operators The exact score and every category value
/ffnutrition maxAllNutrients <player> operators Sets every category to 100
/ffnutrition minAllNutrients <player> operators Sets every category to 0
/ffnutrition setAllNutrients <player> <value> operators Sets every category to a value
/ffnutrition addToCategory <player> <category> <value> operators Adds to one category
/ffnutrition setCategory <player> <category> <value> operators Sets one category
/ffnutrition listUncategorizedFoods [modid] operators Lists foods that have no makeup of their own. Ones sorted into categories by name are marked "(pattern)". Click an entry to copy its ID

Configuration

Settings live in the common config (ffnutrition-common.toml in your config folder). The in-game Mods screen has a Config button on 1.21.1 and newer (on Fabric through Mod Menu); on older versions, edit the file. The options you'll most likely want:

Option Default What it does
initialDefault 50 Starting value of every category
decayDefault 0.00083 How much each category drains per tick
maxHealth 20 Health a perfectly balanced diet works toward
minHealth 6 The least health a bad diet can leave you with
allowEatingWhenFull true Lets you eat with a full hunger bar
nutritionModifier and saturationModifier 4 and 2 How strongly a food's hunger and saturation count toward nutrition
preserveNutritionOnDeath false Keep your nutrition when you respawn
useLevelBasedDecay true Higher experience levels drain nutrition more slowly
useNameBasedDecay false Gives each player a stable, slightly different drain per category, based on their name
useItemNutritionFallback false Gives foods with no makeup a default one instead of nothing
useHungerDecay true Slowly drains hunger and saturation
hideGaugeBelowThreshold and gaugeThreshold false and 0 Hide gauge entries that are fine

There are more options for the gauge colors and position, the max health chat messages and the hunger drain; every option has a comment in the config file.

Supported food mods

Nutrition files ship for vanilla (minecraft) and for these mod IDs: artifacts, benssharks, brewinandchewin, burnt, cold_sweat, corn_delight, create, createaddition, crittersandcompanions, delightfulcreators, enderscape, ends_delight, expandeddelight, farmersdelight, fiahi, minecolonies, moredelight, myths_of_the_sea, netherdepthsupgrade, oceansdelight, rusticdelight and tide. Entries for mods that aren't installed are ignored. Other foods are matched by the built-in name rules; see Datapacks.
